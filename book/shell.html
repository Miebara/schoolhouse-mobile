The thinking errors named in this book belong to the research literature — Asch, Festinger, Tversky and Kahneman, Aronson, Cialdini, Tajfel, Forer, Nisbett and Wilson, Tetlock, Taleb — and the framing belongs to Rolf Dobelli, whose The Art of Thinking Clearly (2011; English 2013) catalogues ninety-nine of them. Nothing here quotes him at length.

An argument, offered as an argument. Chapter 25 is where it is turned on itself.
