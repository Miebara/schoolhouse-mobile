# Broken Wings: A History of Aviation Crises in Nigeria

A complete non-fiction book manuscript, developed through a structured
authoring pipeline (concept → outline → sources → style → drafting → revision).

## Contents

- `planning/` — the working documents behind the manuscript
  - `01-concept.md` — thesis, audience, voice, length, positioning (Loop A)
  - `02-outline.md` — full 4-part, 17-chapter outline with summaries (Loop B)
  - `03-source-matrix.md` — chapter-by-chapter source integration plan (Loop C)
  - `04-style-guide.md` — voice rules, structural template, per-chapter tone samples (Loop D)
- `manuscript/` — the book itself
  - `00-front-matter.md` — title page, dedication, epigraph, author's note, preface, introduction
  - `part-1/` — The Ascent (1925–1990), chapters 1–3
  - `part-2/` — The Crashes (1990–2020), chapters 4–10
  - `part-3/` — The Patterns, chapters 11–14
  - `part-4/` — The Future Flight Path, chapters 15–17
  - `99-back-matter.md` — epilogue, appendices (accident timeline, glossary, abbreviations), notes on sources

## Length

Target: 350–450 printed pages ≈ 90,000–115,000 words of body text.

**Manuscript as completed: ≈ 90,000 words ≈ 350–380 typeset pages**
(front matter ~4,900; Part I ~15,000; Part II ~34,700; Part III
~17,800; Part IV ~11,800; notes and back matter ~6,600). Word counts
per file are reproducible with `wc -w` over `manuscript/`.

## Voice

The prose style is modeled on the calm, analytical, historically panoramic,
comparative essay style associated with writers such as Fareed Zakaria.
The book is an original work; the style is an homage, not an attribution.

## Important note on facts and citations

This manuscript was drafted by an AI writing system from general historical
knowledge. Dates, casualty figures, quotations, and report findings reflect
widely reported accounts but **must be independently verified against primary
sources** (AIB/NSIB accident reports, NCAA records, ICAO audit documents,
contemporaneous press coverage) before publication. The source matrix in
`planning/03-source-matrix.md` lists what to check for each chapter. Passages
that would require verified quotation or archival confirmation are written to
be checkable, not invented in unverifiable detail.
